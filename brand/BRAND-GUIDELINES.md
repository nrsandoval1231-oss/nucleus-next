# OBELISK — The Continuum

## Brand promise
Intelligence in Context.

## Product experience
The Living Present — Everything in context. Nothing out of sight.

## Symbol
Two asymmetrical architectural forms connected by a curved channel. This is an original **production vector interpretation** of the selected concept image, not an exact trace of the generated artwork. The precise final geometry requires visual approval.

## Logo rules
- Prefer the ivory mark on Obsidian for interface headers; champagne for signature moments.
- Use the solid single-color versions for printing, engraving, favicons and small UI.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add drop shadows or put on visually noisy imagery.
- Preserve at least 20% of the mark width as clear space on all sides.
- Below 40 px use symbol only, not the tagline.

## Typography
Cormorant Garamond: editorial/hero. Sora: interface labels and navigation. Inter: body copy. IBM Plex Mono: technical metadata.

## Experience
Cinematic crystal environments belong to product storytelling and hero surfaces; data panels remain high-contrast, calm and legible. The 3D environment is not a replacement for the logo.

## Status and limitations
Visual direction approved, but the supplied SVGs are draft master artwork pending owner review. Font names are specified but fonts are not bundled. Trademark clearance has not been completed. All screenshots and illustrative product metrics are concepts, not evidence of live functionality.
